Submit your own animation — open source

1. CreateHit ＋ Create and draw 1–8 frames on the 5×5 grid. Or paste an existing MakeCode function … basic.showLeds(...) and hit Parse — it extracts frames automatically.
2. Align to the code formatEvery submission must be 5 rows × 5 cols of # / . only, with name, category, speed (50–2000ms). The builder validates this and generates both the MakeCode function and the library entry.
3. SubmitSave to My Creations (stored in your browser), then hit Submit to GitHub — it pre-fills an issue with your library entry. Maintainers merge it into animations.js. See CONTRIBUTING.md.

How to use in 20 seconds

1. CopyHit Copy function on any card. You get a clean function Name() { while(true){ basic.showLeds(...) } }.
2. PasteGo to makecode.microbit.org → switch to JavaScript → paste the function anywhere outside other blocks.
3. Call itInside basic.forever(), onStart, or input.onButtonPressed(Button.A, () => { Name() }) — call it. Flash to your micro:bit.
// Example — paste this, then call Boom()
function Boom() {
    while (true) {
        basic.showLeds(`
            . # . # .
            . # . # .
            . . # . .
            . # . # .
            . # . # .
            `)
        basic.pause(300)
        basic.showLeds(`
            # . . . #
            . # . # .
            . . # . .
            . # . # .
            # . . . #
            `)
        basic.pause(300)
    }
}
input.onButtonPressed(Button.A, function () {
    Boom()
})
